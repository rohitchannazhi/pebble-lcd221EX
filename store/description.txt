A Pebble Time 2 watch face that recreates the look of the Casio W-221H digital watch: a crisp LCD with 7-segment digits, faint "unlit" segments like a real display, a dot-matrix weekday and BT / CHG / DST / MUTE indicators, all between a top and a bottom bezel.

WHAT IT SHOWS
- Time (12 or 24 hour, with the PM marker), weekday and day of the month (MON 05)
- Today's low and high temperature, and the current temperature (C or F, or follow your watch)
- Top bezel: battery level and step count, or your own text instead of either
- Bottom bezel: a heart and your latest heart rate, plus a text label of your choice
- Indicators for phone connection, charging, daylight saving time and Quiet Time

SETTINGS
Live preview. Black or silver case, inverted colors, 7-segment digits or four condensed fonts, divider, window edge and indicator styles, a color for every part of the face, backlight color, and vibration on phone disconnect or reconnect.

PERMISSIONS
Location (through your phone) for the weather, and health data for steps and heart rate. The only thing that leaves your phone is a weather request to Open-Meteo containing your location rounded to about 100 meters.

Pebble Time 2 only.
