New: Digit style: the 7-segment digits, or one of three fonts (Oxanium, Chakra Petch, Wide) for all the text and numbers on the LCD.
New: Divider lines: solid, segmented, ruler ticks, corner brackets or HUD chamfer.

Fixed: with corner brackets, the bottom row's numbers are centred between the brackets.
New: Leading zero in 12-hour time (07:05), with PM in the indicator box in place of CHG.
Changed: the LCD window edges follow the divider line style, and the bottom row is centred between the divider and the bottom of the LCD.

Fixed: with a divider style other than Solid, the plain LCD window edge (visible with custom colors or the silver case) is no longer drawn under the style's own edge.
New: Indicators style: grid, pills, active only or icons.
Changed: the heart rate shows a heart instead of HR; with corner brackets the LCD's corners are bolder, and the ruler-ticks edge is thicker.
Fixed: with a font, the day of the month matches the weekday's size and both are centred on the indicators; the temperature is centred in its box.
Changed: single-digit days and months always have a leading zero (05, 06-05); the Single-digit dates setting is gone.
Fixed: with the Wide digits, the indicator labels (BT, DST...) are legible: they use Oxanium's letters.
New: Min / max marks: tall arrows (new default), triangles (bigger than before) or LO / HI; below zero the minus goes before the number.
New: Window edge style: match the dividers, center tab, notched, circuit or double line.
